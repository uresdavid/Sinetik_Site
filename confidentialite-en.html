Privacy Policy

How we process and protect your data — Swiss nFADP & GDPR.

Provisional version — document under legal review. Framework: Swiss nFADP + GDPR for individuals located in the EU. Authority: FDPIC.

1. Who is responsible?

Sinetik — [entity, see Legal notice] (“we”). Two distinct roles:

  • Account data of users (staff, coaches, independent athletes): Sinetik is the data controller.
  • Athlete data entered/imported by a client organisation (club, federation, clinic): the organisation is the data controller and Sinetik acts as a processor on its behalf (data processing agreement / DPA). Requests regarding this data should be addressed to the organisation.

Contact: .

2. What data do we process?

  • Account & identity: last name, first name, contact email, username, password (hashed), role, organisation, language, login logs.
  • Organisation & subscription: name, type, sport(s), country, subscription status and dates (payments via Stripe).
  • Performance and monitoring: measurements (GPS, VBT, tests, load), wellness, anthropometry, calendar, programmes.
  • Sensitive / health data (art. 5 nFADP): wellness, pain, injuries, medical follow-up — processed with enhanced measures and an appropriate legal basis.
  • Technical: IP address, device/browser, strictly necessary cookies.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing and securing the service — performance of the contract.
  • Managing the subscription and billing — performance of the contract / legal obligation.
  • Processing athletes' health/performance data — consent / organisation's responsibility.
  • Email verification, password reset, notifications — performance of the contract / legitimate interest.
  • Improving and securing the platform — legitimate interest.

4. Recipients and processors

We never sell your data. Technical processors: Supabase (database, auth, storage — hosted in the EU/Ireland), Vercel (USA, app hosting), Stripe (payments — no card data retained by Sinetik), Resend [or other] (transactional emails). Each is bound by a data processing agreement.

5. Transfers outside Switzerland / the EU

The main data (database, auth, storage) is hosted in the EU (Ireland). Some processors (Vercel, Stripe, Resend) may process certain data in the United States, subject to appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses / recognised mechanisms).

6. Retention period

Data is kept for as long as the account / organisation is active. If a subscription is suspended, data is retained to allow reactivation. Permanent deletion occurs on request, or after notice from us, subject to statutory retention periods.

7. Security

Strict isolation per organisation, encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest, role-based access control, hashed passwords, backups.

8. Your rights

In accordance with the nFADP (and the GDPR if you are in the EU): rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection and data release / portability. For data managed by an organisation, please contact it first. To exercise your rights: . You may lodge a complaint with the FDPIC (edoeb.admin.ch) or your data protection authority.

9. Minors

For minor athletes, the processing of data (particularly health data) requires the consent of the legal guardian, collected by the client organisation.

10. Cookies

See the Cookie Policy. By default, only strictly necessary cookies are used.

11. Data breach

In the event of a breach presenting a risk, we notify the FDPIC as soon as possible (72 hours where the GDPR applies) and inform the data subjects where the law requires it.
