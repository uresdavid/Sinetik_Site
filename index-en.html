The problem

Performance data is everywhere. The decisions, nowhere.

Sensors, spreadsheets, apps that don't talk to each other. Tools built for the elite — too expensive, too heavy for the field. The result: we measure a lot, decide on feel, and equip poorly.

The answer

An ecosystem, not a pile of tools.

Four tools designed together: measure on the field, bring it together and decide, coach, equip. One standard, one data thread — from the sensor to the boot.

Bar velocity, repetition by repetition.
