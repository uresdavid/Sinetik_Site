Cookie Policy

What we use, and why.

Provisional version — under legal review.

1. What is a cookie?

A small file placed on your device when you visit. We also include the browser's local storage in the same category.

2. Our principle

By default, we only use cookies that are strictly necessary for operation and security. No advertising cookies. Any audience-measurement or non-essential preference cookie is only placed with your consent, which can be withdrawn at any time.

3. Strictly necessary cookies

  • Authentication session — keeping you securely signed in.
  • Scope & subscription (Studio app) — remembering the selection and access state (short duration).
  • Language — remembering your language preference.
  • Theme — remembering light/dark mode.

Indicative list — to be completed before final publication.

4. Cookies subject to consent

To date: none. Any audience-measurement tool added later will be listed here and subject to your prior consent.

5. Managing your cookies

Through the consent banner (for non-essential cookies) and through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent sign-in.
