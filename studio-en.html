How it gets set up

  1. We listen A conversation to understand your organisation, your age groups, your working habits.
  2. We configure Studio is set up for your organisation: your teams, your tests, your metrics, your vocabulary.
  3. We import what exists Your spreadsheet history and GPS exports are brought in — you do not start from scratch.
  4. We support you Onboarding with the staff, then regular check-ins through the first season.

An organisation with three data sources is up and running in one to two weeks. On your side: your squad list and one export per source. On ours: configuration, migration of your history, and onboarding with the staff.

04

What Studio does not do

Everyone will tell you what their tool can do. Here is what ours does not — so there are no surprises come January.

  1. It does not predict injuries Nobody can do that reliably. Studio shows you what is changing, what falls outside your own thresholds, what keeps recurring. The decision stays yours.
  2. It does not replace your eye The data says a player ran four hundred metres less than usual. It does not say he slept badly. You do.
  3. It does not measure anything itself Studio aggregates what your sensors produce. You keep your GPS, your force platforms and your current tools.
  4. It does not hand you ready-made programmes It gives you the tools to build your own. Your method is your value; it is not a software vendor's job to sell it to you.

06

What we get asked most

Do we have to drop our current tools?

No. Studio sits on top of what you already use. You carry on with your GPS, your force platforms, your questionnaires. We take in their exports.

How long does it take to get started?

It depends on the number of sources. In practice: athletes and teams imported on day one, then one import profile per source. An organisation with three sources is up and running in one to two weeks, onboarding included.

Does it work for sports other than football?

Yes. Nothing in the platform is football-specific: metrics, vocabulary and thresholds are defined by each organisation. Football is simply the sport where the tool has been tested hardest.

Who sees what?

You define the roles and what each one sees. Medical data can be restricted to medical staff.

And if we want to stop?

You export everything and you leave. No exit fees, no proprietary format.
